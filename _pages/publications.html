B.Black Holes Photon Ring Holography

We investigated the holographic properties of Reduced Kiselev Black Holes under Prof. Tower Wang's supervision (ECNU). Our main focus was on symmetry analysis, extending the original findings of identical symmetry groups in the near-horizon limit to different black hole. We explored the observational properties of Reduced Kiselev Black Holes within the context of black hole accretion disk dynamics and holographic duality, incorporating Strominger's insights on the holography of the photon ring.

Here, I summarize key aspects of our work:

  1. Extending to Kiselev black holes resulted in trivial outcomes.
  2. Reviewed the construction of symmetries.
  3. Demonstrated that our model does not yield non-trivial results.
C.Particle Experiment

Under Prof Kim Siang (TDLI)'s instruction, our group did the feasibility study of Muon creation using high-energy electron beam from SHINE facility. Details of my work in this project are as follows.

  • Utilized Geant4-based software musrSim to simulate high-energy particle beam interactions, optimizing target materials and beam parameters to enhance muon yield and streamline muon beam lines.
  • Configured data oscilloscopes and LaBr3 experimental equipment, preparing data acquisition codes for preliminary experiments at SXFEL.
